# Content Strategy

*Last updated: 2026-05-10*
*Source of truth for ShotStudio blog/content pages. Reads `.agents/product-marketing.md`.*

## Goal
Pull indie iOS developers into the funnel via search before they evaluate competitors. Primary KPI: organic sessions → sign-up. Secondary: cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity for "best App Store screenshot tool" queries.

## Content Pillars (4)

| # | Pillar | Owns the question | Buyer stage |
|---|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| 1 | **App Store launch playbook** | "I'm submitting my first app — what do I need?" | Awareness |
| 2 | **Screenshot craft & conversion** | "Why aren't my screenshots converting?" | Awareness → Consideration |
| 3 | **Tools, alternatives & comparisons** | "Which screenshot tool should I pick?" | Consideration → Decision |
| 4 | **Indie design shortcuts (anti-Figma)** | "How do I do this without learning design?" | Awareness, brand voice |

Each pillar maps cleanly onto the ICP's actual pain — submission anxiety, conversion anxiety, tool fatigue, design overwhelm.

## Topic Clusters

### Pillar 1 — App Store launch playbook
- App Store Connect walkthrough (first-time submitters)
- App Store screenshot specs 2026 (1290×2796, sRGB, no transparency)
- Required vs optional App Store assets (icon, screenshots, preview video)
- App Store review rejection reasons related to assets
- Submission checklist: night-before-launch
- App Store Connect screenshot upload errors (cluster of niche fixes)

### Pillar 2 — Screenshot craft & conversion
- What makes an App Store screenshot convert (with public-data analysis)
- A/B testing screenshots via App Store Connect product page experiments
- Hero shot vs feature shot vs lifestyle shot — when to use each
- Screenshot copywriting: 1-line headlines that work
- Screenshot mistakes that hurt conversion (raw simulator dumps, walls of text, etc.)
- Localized screenshots: should you bother?

### Pillar 3 — Tools, alternatives & comparisons
- Best App Store screenshot generators 2026 (curation page; positions ShotStudio + alternatives honestly)
- ShotStudio vs AppMockUp (and per-competitor — see programmatic-seo.md)
- AppMockUp alternatives / Previewed alternatives / Rotato alternatives (per-competitor)
- Free vs paid screenshot tools — when each makes sense
- Subscription vs one-time-pay screenshot tools (positioning piece)

### Pillar 4 — Indie design shortcuts
- App Store screenshots without Figma
- 5 indie iOS apps with great screenshots (and why they work)
- The minimum-viable App Store listing
- Stop overthinking your screenshots — ship the v1

## Format & cadence

- All posts under `/blog/[slug]` — flat structure, not hub-and-spoke (overkill at this stage).
- Length: 1,200–2,000 words. No fluff intros. Lead with answer.
- Every post includes: 1290×2796 spec mention, internal link to pricing, contextual CTA matching the pillar.
- Tone: per `.agents/product-marketing.md` — confident, dry, declarative. Senior indie talking to a peer.
- Cadence: 2 posts/week sustainable for one person; ship the priority 12 first, then layer pillars 2 and 3 deeper.

## Voice-of-customer language to keep using

From `.agents/product-marketing.md` — verbatim phrases to mirror:
- "I just need screenshots for the App Store."
- "I don't want to pay a subscription for something I'll use once."
- "I'm not a designer."
- "I'm submitting tomorrow."

## What's deliberately NOT in scope

- Android / Google Play content (anti-persona)
- Enterprise / team / agency content (anti-persona)
- Generic "AI image generation" content (cannibalizes the niche positioning)
- Non-screenshot mobile-marketing topics (ASO copy, app icon design) — too broad; revisit later
