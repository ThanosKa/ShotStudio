# Pricing — ShotStudio

ShotStudio sells **one-time credit packs**. There is no subscription. 1 credit = 1 generation set = 3 polished App Store screenshots at 1290×2796 (one per source screen the user uploads).

## Starter
- Price: $7 USD (one-time)
- Includes: 2 generation sets (6 images)
- Features: All 4 style presets, AI-written headline.

## Growth (most chosen)
- Price: $17 USD (one-time)
- Includes: 5 generation sets (15 images)
- Features: Same as Starter. Best per-set value if you A/B test copy or hero angles.

## Studio
- Price: $37 USD (one-time)
- Includes: 12 generation sets (36 images)
- Features: Same as Growth. Lowest per-set price — built for indie portfolios and small studios shipping a few apps a year.

## What every plan includes
- 1290×2796 PNG output (iPhone 6.7" portrait, the App Store Connect-required spec).
- Four style presets: Soft & Bright, Dark & Premium, Clean & Minimal, Bold & Playful.
- AI-written headline from your pitch and audience.
- Click-to-edit text on the preview screen.
- Credits never expire.

## Notes
- VAT / sales tax is calculated at checkout via Stripe Tax.
- Coupon codes (including 100%-off partnership codes) apply on the Stripe Checkout page.
- All sales are final. Credits are refunded automatically only when image generation fails after our retry/fallback path is exhausted.
