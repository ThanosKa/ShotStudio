## Competitor Landscape Overview

The App Store screenshot tools market splits into three clear groups:

1. **Subscription template editors** — Screenshots.pro ($19–$49/mo), Previewed Pro ($228/yr), Shotbot (iOS subscription). Charge recurring for what most indies do once or twice a year.
2. **Free template editors** — AppMockUp (free forever, donation-funded). Maximum-friction-removal on price; maximum-friction in the manual editing workflow.
3. **Premium one-time desktop apps** — Rotato (~$59–$199), Previewed Plus ($9.99). One-time payments but heavy tools optimized for designers/3D work, not "ship tonight" indies.

**The pattern**: every existing tool is a *template editor*. None generate the screenshot from scratch. None write the headlines. None auto-match the user's app palette. The user is still doing the design work — they're just doing it inside a friendlier UI.

**ShotStudio's wedge**: It's the only tool that runs the screenshot *generation*, not screenshot *editing*. The competitor set has not yet adopted gpt-image-class generation as a primary workflow. This window is the moat.

## Key Takeaways (Strategic)

1. **AI generation is an uncontested wedge.** Not one of 5 direct competitors generates screenshots from scratch with image-model AI. They all edit templates. As soon as one does, ShotStudio's primary moat is at risk — speed of move matters.

2. **Subscription fatigue is real and the category is wide open for one-time pay.** Three of five (Screenshots.pro, Previewed Pro, Shotbot) are subscription. Two (Rotato, Previewed Plus) already validated one-time pay works at $9.99 → $199 range. ShotStudio's $7 floor is the lowest one-time entry in the category.

3. **"Free" (AppMockUp) is the hardest competitor to displace, not the most expensive.** They own 7M+ screenshots of usage and the "free app screenshot generator" search intent. The win is *speed and AI*, not price — $7 vs. $0 is acceptable friction if the time savings are real.

4. **No competitor writes copy.** Shotbot offers caption *guidance* (data-driven hints), but none generate the headline from the user's pitch. This is a clean, narrow, demonstrable wedge for ShotStudio marketing.

5. **Logo walls dominate credibility in this category.** Screenshots.pro (Google, Reddit), Rotato (Adobe, Google, Amazon). ShotStudio has none pre-launch. **Highest-leverage marketing investment in the first 90 days: capture 3–5 named indie/dev testimonials with real handles**, even before paid logos appear.
