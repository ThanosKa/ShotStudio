## Competitive Implications for ShotStudio

**Where they're strong vs. us**: API, localization, multi-device "panoramic" outputs, established logos.

**Where we're strong vs. them**:
- **One-time pay vs. their subscription** — their $19/mo for indies-once-a-year purchasers is exactly the pain ShotStudio targets
- **Speed** — ShotStudio takes 3 inputs and outputs in ~1 minute. Screenshots.pro requires layout editing.
- **AI-generated headlines + theme/palette auto-sampling** — they require manual design choices
- **Privacy-first / zero image persistence** — they have cloud storage (the opposite stance)
- **No design skill required** — they still expect users to lay things out

**Opportunities**:
- Direct comparison page ("Screenshots.pro alternative — one-time pay, AI-styled") — high-intent SEO target
- Lead with "I forgot to cancel" pain point against their subscription
- Position the AI-written headlines as the "no copywriter, no layout" wedge they don't have

**Threats**:
- Strong feature depth (3D, API, localization) appeals to teams who eventually want more
- Bigger brand logos build initial credibility ShotStudio lacks pre-launch
