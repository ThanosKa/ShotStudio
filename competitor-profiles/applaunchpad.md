## Positioning vs ShotStudio

- Template editor: the user uploads screens, picks device frames and types titles. Its AI is translation and resizing, not headline writing or layout.
- Wins over ShotStudio: Google Play and iPad output, localization, large template library, free tier.
- Loses to ShotStudio for the iPhone-only indie: subscription vs $7 one-time, manual layout and copy, projects stored in the account.
