## Positioning vs ShotStudio

- Editor with AI assistance: AI caption suggestions and restyling, but the user builds the layout.
- Wins over ShotStudio: localization, Google Play / iPad / Watch / Mac sizes, direct store upload, PPO/CPP variants, free tier.
- Loses to ShotStudio for the iPhone-only indie: $25/mo or $99/yr subscription vs $7 one-time, manual layout, projects stored in the account.
