## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- Strongest brand wall in the category (Adobe, Google, Amazon, Uber)
- 200K+ users is real, quantified social proof
- True 3D engine — actually different from 2D-template competitors
- One-time pay model already (no subscription objection)
- Figma plugin = designer workflow lock-in
- Up-market premium positioning

### Weaknesses
- Desktop app required — friction vs. browser tools
- Premium pricing (historically $99–$199) far above ShotStudio
- Steep for a one-time App Store job — overbuilt for indies
- Aimed at designers/agencies, not solo indies
- Static screenshots not their primary use case anymore — video/animation focus
- Free trial is unlimited (no urgency), but conversion to paid is unclear

## Competitive Implications for ShotStudio

**Where they're strong vs. us**:
- Premium positioning + brand wall
- 3D + animation + video capabilities (we don't do)
- Designer workflow (Figma plugin)
- Already validated one-time-pay model at scale

**Where we're strong vs. them**:
- **Price floor** — $7 vs their $59+ entry tier
- **No download required** — browser-based
- **AI-generated** — they require manual 3D scene composition
- **Indie focus** — they sell to designers/agencies
- **Speed** — 1 minute vs. their "minutes" with a learning curve
- **No design skill needed** — they assume designer-level taste

**Opportunities**:
- "Rotato alternative for indies who just need App Store screenshots" — high-intent comparison
- Position as the indie tier below their Basic
- Win on "I don't need 3D animation, I need 3 screenshots tonight"

**Threats**:
- If they release an indie-tier product, our positioning is at risk
- Their brand credibility is hard to match without time + users
- Designers and agencies serving indies might recommend Rotato over ShotStudio
