## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- Native Apple integration (Share Sheet, Shortcuts) — friction-free for iOS-only devs
- Caption guidance is a real differentiator (data-driven copywriting hints)
- visionOS support early
- iCloud sync = sticky once adopted

### Weaknesses
- iOS/macOS only — Android devs excluded
- Web is blocked / weak — losing SEO discovery
- Manual framing — no AI generation
- Subscription on a one-shot job (same pain as Screenshots.pro)
- Free tier daily limit creates friction at exactly the moment a user is trying to ship

---

## Competitive Implications for ShotStudio

**Where they're strong vs. us**:
- Native Apple workflow integrations (Share Sheet, Shortcuts) we'll never match
- Caption guidance based on top-app analysis — closer to our AI-headline thesis
- Tight Apple-platform polish

**Where we're strong vs. them**:
- **Web access** — works for any indie regardless of OS
- **AI-generated layouts + headlines** — they only frame + caption
- **One-time pay** — they subscribe
- **No daily limits** — they cap free
- **Theme/palette auto-derive** — they use static templates
- **Privacy-first** — they iCloud-sync, we persist nothing

**Opportunities**:
- Target indies who've hit the Shotbot daily limit at submission crunch time
- Position the AI generation as "Shotbot caption guidance, but for the whole screenshot"
- Win on Android-supporting devs they can't serve (note: ShotStudio v1 is also iOS-only output — verify before pushing this angle)

**Threats**:
- Native Apple integrations are sticky — once an iOS dev wires Shotbot into Shortcuts, switching cost is real
- Caption guidance feature could expand into AI-written copy (direct overlap with our wedge)
