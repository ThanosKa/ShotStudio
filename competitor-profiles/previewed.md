## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- $9.99 one-time Plus tier is the closest direct competitor to ShotStudio's positioning
- 3D + video output is genuinely differentiated
- Covers multiple use cases (App Store + social + video) — broader than ShotStudio
- "Exports stay forever" is a clean message

### Weaknesses
- Lite tier requires CC attribution — friction users hate
- $228/yr Pro is expensive given Plus exists at $9.99
- No social proof / no customer logos — weak credibility signal
- Still requires manual mockup setup — no AI generation
- "Pixel perfect mockups" claim is undermined by the manual workflow

## Competitive Implications for ShotStudio

**Where they're strong vs. us**:
- Already has a one-time pay option ($9.99 Plus) — the closest pricing parallel
- 3D + animation + video capabilities (we don't do video)
- Broader use-case coverage (social mockups, promo videos)

**Where we're strong vs. them**:
- **AI generation** — they require manual design
- **Speed (1 minute)** — they require building mockup scenes
- **Theme/palette auto-derive from uploads** — they use generic templates
- **AI-written headlines** — they don't write copy for you
- **No CC attribution friction** — we don't have that tier
- **Privacy-first** — they have cloud storage built into the model

**Opportunities**:
- Direct head-to-head: "Previewed alternative — AI does the layout"
- Win on speed even though pricing is comparable ($7 vs $9.99)
- Lean on "no manual mockup work" as the wedge

**Threats**:
- Most pricing-aligned competitor — their $9.99 one-time captures the same anti-subscription buyer
- If they add AI generation, positioning collapses
- Their broader scope (video, social) could expand to capture more indie wallet share
