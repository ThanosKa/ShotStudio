# ShotStudio — Product Requirements Document

> One-time-pay AI App Store screenshot generator. Upload 3 mobile screenshots, get a polished 4-image App Store set powered by `gpt-image-2`.

## Problem Statement

Indie developers and small teams shipping apps to the App Store routinely hit a friction point at submission: their listing needs polished marketing screenshots, but they lack design skills, time, or budget for a designer or a Figma session. Existing tools like `appshots.me` lock value behind monthly subscriptions, which mismatches the actual usage pattern — most indie devs need this 1–10 times per app launch, not continuously. Subscription dread leads many to ship amateur-looking listings, hurting App Store conversion. With image generation models like OpenAI's `gpt-image-2` (released April 2026) now producing brand-quality outputs from minimal input, there is space for a one-time-pay product that lets indie devs ship App Store-quality screenshots in under a minute, with no recurring billing, no design skills, no Figma.

## Solution

ShotStudio is a SaaS at `shotstudio` that turns 3 raw mobile screenshots plus minimal app context into a polished 4-image App Store screenshot set. The set follows a proven 4-shot template (1 title/value-prop hero + 3 device-framed feature shots) backed by industry A/B test data. The product is single-tenant (individual user accounts; no organizations), pay-once via credit packs (no subscriptions), and privacy-first (uploaded screenshots and generated images are never persisted server-side). Three credit pack tiers — Starter $7 (1 set), Growth $17 (4 sets), Studio $37 (10 sets) — cover the full indie spectrum from "I'm shipping one app" to "I run a small portfolio." The differentiation against `appshots.me` is explicit: one-time payment, true privacy, no historical storage, faster onboarding (1 minute vs. 3+ minutes of style dropdowns), and a consolidated 4-preset style picker instead of 5 separate aesthetic decisions.

## User Stories

1. As a visitor, I can browse the marketing landing page and see real example sets generated by ShotStudio so I can judge output quality before signing up.
2. As a visitor, I can read the pricing page showing all three credit packs (Starter $7 / Growth $17 / Studio $37) with what each includes.
3. As a visitor, I can sign up with Google or email via Clerk in under 30 seconds.
4. As a new user, I receive a welcome email via Resend immediately after signup.
5. As a new user, I land on the Home page (sidebar nav: Home + Settings) and see a clear "Buy credits to begin" call-to-action because I start with 0 credits.
6. As a user, I can click a credit pack and be redirected to a Stripe Checkout page.
7. As a user, I can apply a discount or 100%-off coupon code at Stripe Checkout.
8. As a user, after Stripe Checkout succeeds I am returned to the app and my credit balance updates.
9. As a user, I receive a purchase confirmation email after Stripe payment succeeds.
10. As a user with credits, I can click "New screenshot set" and begin a 3-step input flow.
11. As a user, in Step 1 I provide my app name (required), tagline (required, 5–10 words, used as the headline on Shot 1), and category (required dropdown: productivity, wellness, finance, games, social, education, lifestyle, dev tools, other).
12. As a user, in Step 1 I can optionally upload a logo image to be used on Shot 1.
13. As a user, in Step 2 I drag-drop 3 mobile screenshots into role-labeled slots: "Hero feature," "Differentiator," and "Another feature."
14. As a user, in Step 2 I can optionally type a per-shot headline (3–6 words, verb-led); if blank the AI will generate one I can edit later.
15. As a user, in Step 3 I see four style preset cards as visual thumbnails: Soft & Bright, Dark & Premium, Clean & Minimal, Bold & Playful.
16. As a user, the style preset is auto-selected based on my chosen category (e.g., wellness → Soft & Bright, finance → Dark & Premium), and I can override.
17. As a user, when I click Generate, exactly one credit is debited from my balance immediately.
18. As a user, I see a progress UI for ~30–90 seconds while four image-generation calls run in parallel via OpenRouter to `gpt-image-2`.
19. As a user, if all four generations succeed I am taken to a preview screen showing the four images.
20. As a user, on the preview screen I can click any text element on any image (tagline on Shot 1, headlines on Shots 2–4) and inline-edit it before downloading.
21. As a user, on the preview screen I can click "Regenerate this shot" on any image to re-run that single shot at no additional credit cost.
22. As a user, when I am satisfied I click "Download all" and four PNG files are saved to my device.
23. As a user, my uploaded screenshots and generated images are not stored on ShotStudio's servers; once my session closes the assets are gone (this is communicated as a marketing benefit and codified in the Privacy Policy).
24. As a user, if any of the four generations fails the system retries that shot once; if it still fails, my credit is automatically refunded and I see a clear error.
25. As a user, if OpenRouter is fully unavailable, the Generate button is disabled with a "Service temporarily unavailable" banner and no credit is debited.
26. As a user, I am rate-limited via Upstash Redis to a configurable cap (e.g., 20 generations per hour) to prevent abuse.
27. As a user, I cannot upload anything other than PNG/JPEG images, and individual file size is capped (e.g., 10 MB) with clear validation errors.
28. As a user, I can navigate to Settings and see my current credit balance, my email, and a button "Manage billing" that opens the Stripe Customer Portal.
29. As a user, in the Stripe Customer Portal I can view receipts, invoices, and update my email or payment method.
30. As a user, I cannot request refunds inside ShotStudio (the ToS specifies all sales final); any disputes are handled directly through Stripe.
31. As a user, I can sign out from the sidebar at any time.
32. As a user, navigating to an invalid route returns a styled 404 page with a "Go home" link.
33. As a user, all output images conform to the App Store iPhone 6.7" portrait specification (1290×2796 PNG, sRGB, no transparency).
34. As an operator, OpenRouter and Stripe usage are monitored against budget caps configured in those vendors' dashboards.

## Implementation Decisions

### Modules

**Auth.** Authentication is delegated entirely to Clerk. A Clerk webhook on user creation triggers a row insert into the `users` table in Postgres and dispatches the welcome email via Resend. ShotStudio code never handles passwords, sessions, or social-login flows directly.

**Billing.** Stripe Checkout (hosted) for one-time credit purchases; Stripe Customer Portal (hosted) for receipts and payment-method management. Stripe Tax is enabled to handle VAT/sales tax. Coupons are enabled at the Stripe level, including 100%-off codes for promotional partnerships. The `checkout.session.completed` webhook is the single event consumed; idempotency is enforced by storing the Stripe event ID in Upstash Redis with a TTL before processing.

**Credits.** User credit balance is a single integer column on the `users` table. Atomic operations are exposed: `debit(userId, n)` and `refund(userId, n)`, each wrapping a Postgres transaction that updates `users.credits` and inserts a row into `transactions`. The credit accounting policy is *debit-before-AI-call, refund-on-failure*: the credit is removed when the generation job starts and only restored if the system fails to deliver four valid images. This is safer than debit-after because long-running AI calls can drop the connection, leaving the user paid for output they never received.

**Generation.** A single deep module exposes one interface: `generate(input) → outputUrls[4]`. Internally it (1) validates the input shape, (2) constructs four prompt templates derived from the chosen style preset and the user-provided context, (3) fires four parallel calls to OpenRouter's `gpt-image-2` endpoint using `Promise.allSettled` so partial failures do not cancel siblings, (4) retries any single failed shot once with exponential backoff plus jitter, (5) returns the four image data URLs to the caller. The HTTP route running this module declares `export const maxDuration = 300` so Vercel allows the long-running call to complete.

**Job status.** Each generation creates a row in `generations` (status: `pending` | `complete` | `failed`). Generated image URLs are *not* persisted; they are returned only to the originating browser session. The frontend polls `GET /api/generations/[id]` every 2 seconds during processing. When `status = complete` the response carries the four temporary base64 image URLs for in-browser display. Once the user downloads or closes the tab, the URLs are unrecoverable.

**Email.** Resend handles two transactional emails: a welcome message (triggered by the Clerk user-creation webhook) and a purchase-confirmation message (triggered by the Stripe `checkout.session.completed` webhook). No marketing emails in v1.

**Rate limiting.** Upstash Redis sliding-window rate limit keyed on Clerk user ID. The default cap (e.g., 20 generations per hour) is configurable via env var. Returns HTTP 429 with retry-after metadata when exceeded.

**Error monitoring.** Vercel's built-in logs + the `generations` table (status/failure_reason rows) are the operator surface in v1. OpenRouter cost monitoring is handled through OpenRouter's native dashboard with a hard spending cap configured upstream.

```
POST /api/generations
  body:    {
    appName: string,
    tagline: string,
    category: string,
    logo?: base64,
    screenshots: [base64, base64, base64],
    headlines?: [string?, string?, string?],
    stylePreset: 'soft_bright' | 'dark_premium' | 'clean_minimal' | 'bold_playful'

  returns: { generationId: string }

GET /api/generations/:id

POST /api/generations/:id/regenerate

  side-effects: NO additional credit debit
```

### Style presets

Four presets, each a server-side configuration object that bundles a color palette, typography stack, backdrop pattern, headline placement, and a voice/tone modifier injected into the AI prompt:

1. **Soft & Bright** — pastel gradients, rounded sans, warm tone. Default for wellness, lifestyle, social, dating.
2. **Dark & Premium** — charcoal/black with single bright accent, sharp Inter, professional tone. Default for finance, crypto, dev tools.
3. **Clean & Minimal** — white/off-white, single accent, restrained typography. Default for productivity, education, utilities.
4. **Bold & Playful** — saturated solid colors, oversized display type, sticker energy. Default for games, creator tools, kids apps.

Smart defaults map category → preset; user can override at Step 3.

### Output shape and dimensions

Each "set" delivers exactly 4 PNG files at 1290×2796 portrait (App Store iPhone 6.7" requirement). `gpt-image-2` natively outputs at 1024×1536; a server-side image processing step (using `sharp`) upscales to 1290×2796 before returning to the client. No watermarks at any tier.

## Testing Decisions

- **External-behavior focus.** Tests target API endpoints and emitted side-effects (DB rows, Stripe webhook responses, credit balance changes), not internal AI prompt construction.
- **Stripe webhook idempotency.** Replay every webhook event twice in tests; assert exactly one credit-package addition.
- **Credit accounting safety.** Property-style tests over the debit/refund pair: random sequences of debits and refunds must always leave `users.credits >= 0` and `sum(transactions.amount) == users.credits`.
- **Generation failure paths.** Mock OpenRouter to return (a) all-success, (b) 1-of-4 failure, (c) all-failure, (d) timeout. Assert that the user-visible state and credit balance are correct in each case.
- **Rate-limit boundary.** Send N+1 requests in N seconds; assert HTTP 429 on the last.
- **Manual visual QA before launch.** Generate 50 sample sets across all 4 presets and at least 6 categories. Reject the launch if fewer than ~85% of generated sets are visually shippable. Iterate prompt templates until the bar is hit. This is *the* gate on going public; the engineering is straightforward, the prompt quality is the product.
- **Production monitoring.** Vercel logs for runtime errors. The `generations` table is queried periodically for `status = 'failed'` rate trends; if it crosses a threshold, the launch is paused.

## Out of Scope (v1)

- Google Play Store output formats (separate feature graphic, no device frames). v2.
- Multi-device variants (iPad, multiple iPhone screen sizes, Android sizes). v2.
- Persistent generation history UI. Privacy-first decision; no images stored.
- Free tier or watermarked previews. Pricing strategy is paywall-first.
- Free-text style prompts ("describe the vibe in your own words"). v2 power-user feature.
- Team accounts / multi-user organizations / shared credit pools.
- Public API access for developers.
- Subscription billing of any kind.
- Refund or dispute UI; all sales are final per the ToS.
- Logo design service (we accept logos, we don't make them).
- Translations / i18n; v1 is English-only.
- Dark mode for the ShotStudio dashboard itself (the *generated* style presets include dark variants).
- Public examples gallery sourced from real user generations (privacy conflict).
- A/B-testing infrastructure for the user's own listings (separate adjacent product).

## Further Notes

- **`gpt-image-2` native sizes** are 1024×1024, 1024×1536, 1536×1024. The product requires App Store 1290×2796 portrait. The upscale boundary lives in the Generation module, not the AI prompt.
- **Text rendering.** `gpt-image-2` produces good but imperfect text; expected ~10–15% of generations may produce wonky tagline/headline rendering. Mitigation is two-layered: (1) click-to-edit on the preview screen for every text element, (2) free regeneration of any individual shot.
- **Cost per set** at high quality is roughly ~$0.85 in OpenRouter API plus ~$0.45–$1.30 in Stripe fees per transaction. Margins land at roughly 70–85% across all three tiers.
- **The 4-shot composition** (1 title + 3 device-framed) is data-supported. SplitMetrics aggregate data shows the first screenshot drives 15–30% of conversion; Lab Cave A/B tests show banner-style first screenshots beat raw-UI first screenshots in 3/3 published tests. The product opinionatedly enforces this template rather than letting users freely arrange shots — opinionation is part of the value.
- **Privacy as marketing.** The "your screenshots are never stored on our servers" guarantee is a deliberate marketing line that doubles as a compliance simplifier (no GDPR data-deletion requests for image data, no DMCA exposure on stored content).
- **No-refund policy.** Codified in the Terms of Service to reduce dispute load. Stripe's own dispute mechanism is the only escalation channel.
- **Coupon strategy.** 100%-off codes enable creator/influencer partnerships and beta testing without requiring a separate "free trial" feature.
- **Critical pre-launch concern.** Prompt template quality is the single biggest determinant of product viability. A working app with weak prompts is a non-shippable product. Plan ~2–3 days of dedicated prompt iteration with real test sets immediately after the core scaffolding is in place, and gate launch on the manual-QA pass rate described in Testing Decisions.
- **Why now.** `gpt-image-2` (April 2026) is the first widely available text-rendering image model where output quality is App Store-shippable without hand-touching every result. Earlier models (`gpt-image-1`, FLUX) require text overlay tricks. This window is the product opportunity.
